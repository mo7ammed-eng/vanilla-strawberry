# Project Overview — Vanilla & Strawberry

## Product
A premium ice-cream and dessert restaurant platform combining a public brand website, product catalog, ordering, QR table service, Chef operations, and Admin management.

## Brand promise
Fresh, welcoming, elegant, and simple. The experience should make products and photography the focus.

## Account roles
Exactly three: Customer, Chef, Admin. No additional account role without explicit approval.

## Main experiences
- Public landing and restaurant story.
- Location and photo catalog.
- New arrivals, best sellers, budget-friendly and premium product collections.
- Search, product detail, customization, and cart.
- QR table ordering.
- Online delivery ordering.
- Chef order preparation workflow.
- Admin operations and content management.
- Optional reservations and coupons controlled by Admin.

## Homepage order
1. Landing introduction
2. 3D hero (progressive enhancement)
3. About Us
4. Restaurant location
5. Book-like photo catalog
6. New Arrivals
7. Best Sellers
8. Cheapest Products
9. Premium Products
10. Search/discovery
11. Login/register entry
12. Footer

Avoid repeating the same product collection or content block unnecessarily.

## Important open decisions
Document and resolve only when implementation depends on them:
- Whether product details require login or only checkout does.
- Guest checkout policy.
- Payment methods and payment provider.
- Delivery operating model and source of courier location.
- Reservation time-slot and table-allocation policy.
- Currency, tax, and service-charge rules.
